# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Casual players on a phone or laptop, playing in short bursts (waiting in line, between tasks). They should understand the game within 10 seconds, without instructions beyond one line.

## Product Purpose
ORBIT is a one-tap rhythm and timing game: a ship rides a ring and you tap as it meets the target. Success is "one more try": short runs, instant retry, and a clear next goal (rank, best score, eventually a leaderboard).

## Brand Personality
Electric, kinetic, precise. Geometry Wars energy: neon vector wireframes on black, a warp grid that ripples with every explosion and every kick drum, sparks everywhere, and music that builds as you play. Every hit should feel like detonating something.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI canvas games: dark gradient background, neon glow orbs, system-font all-caps labels.
- Plain synth beeps as sound design.
- Templated menu/game-over screens.

## Design Principles
1. The beat is the clock. Visuals, orb arrivals and feedback are driven by the music's tempo, so syncing to real tracks later is a drop-in.
2. Progress you can hear and see. Each rank changes the colour world and adds a music layer.
3. One point per hit. Effects reward skill, never inflate the score.
4. Retry is instant. Nothing stands between death and the next attempt.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no shake, no beat pulsing of the background). Avoid full-screen strobing. Text keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast via dark outlines. Mute toggle always available.
